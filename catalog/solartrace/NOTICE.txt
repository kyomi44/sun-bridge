Changes made by Sun Bridge
-------------------------
This derivative selects three data worksheets: "AHJ-Utility Timelines",
"AHJ permits reqs", and "Utility IX reqs". It also retains the "Information"
worksheet's definitions and acknowledgments for provenance. Their raw row
values, headers, and missing-value markers are preserved in
catalog/solartrace/rows.json.gz; catalog/solartrace/manifest.json records the
snapshot's provenance. The raw workbook is not bundled. A separate SQL
representation normalizes selected data into entities, historical benchmarks,
and requirements. State-level results and quartile results are excluded from
this first catalog import; it is not a reproduction of the entire workbook.
These transformations are not new observations, official agency verification,
current permit statuses, or commitments about future approval times. Source
ambiguities and vintage information remain documented in
docs/solartrace-sources.md.
